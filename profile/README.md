## Research

### **Filipino Loanword Adaptation**
The Philippines recognizes both Filipino and English as official languages, and both are commonly used in overlapping contexts such as education, business, and media. However, English is rarely the first language of Filipinos, which makes code-switching and phonological adaptation a frequent phenomenon. This linguistic reality motivates the task of computationally modeling how English words are adapted to fit Filipino phonology.

**Topics/Keywords**: Optimality Theory (and its Intractability); Formal Methods (Attribute Grammars); (Filipino, Computational, Generative) Phonology.
